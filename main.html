How to play

Your guns fire on their own. You move, dodge and pick upgrades.

Hot keys

WASD / arrowsMove. Guns auto-fire at the nearest bot.
Shift / SpaceAbility 1 (dash)
Q E CAbilities 2, 3 and 4
XUltimate (charges from kills)
1 2 3Pick level-up cards, or fire the bonus abilities you bought
BOpen or close the shop during shop time
NAlmanac
P / EscPause
TouchDrag anywhere on the left to move. Right thumb: big button = Dash, small = abilities. Tap a skill to auto-aim, or hold and drag it to aim (release to cast, drag back to cancel).

Tips

  • Crates and barrels are solid, and rival agents' shots stop on them, so use cover.
  • Stand inside a lettered site ring to plant the spike, then run before it blows.
  • Grab glowing orbs for ult charge, shield and health, and collect the gold coins bots drop.
  • Rival agents show up from far away, so watch the edge arrows.
  • Max out an ability or weapon (Lv5) to unlock gold Legendary upgrades, up to 3 per run. Purple Epic cards can also appear on level-up.
  • Each season ends with a boss. Other bosses roam in at random and drop relic chests with artifacts (up to 6).
  • After a season boss falls it is shop time: the clock stops and you can spend coins on upgrades, bonus abilities and artifacts. Start the next season when you are ready.
  • The Almanac starts empty and fills up as you meet new things.
